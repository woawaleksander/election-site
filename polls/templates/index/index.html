The Website by Students for Students

About the website

Our goal as your peers and potential class officers is to provide EVERYONE with a voice. If Alex Brandt and I are elected, we'd use this website to allow everyone to do things like vote on snacks, bake sales and more via polls, create suggestions for clubs and events, and more" - Quentin Heng 2026

Why the website?

It was brought to our attention that many have felt under represented the last two years, confused on what role our class officers actually played. That's why we went above and beyond and created this website; to ensure everyone who would like to be kept in the loop, can be (without needing social media or connections to us directly).

Class of 2028 Election

If our ideologies and motives have peaked your interest, or if you'd like a change for a brighter high school career, vote Quentin Heng and Alex Brandt for your 2028 class presidents. Following our victory, we'll continue to work on finalizing site, and making decisions that benefit our entire class as a whole.
